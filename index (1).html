AGGREGATE SUPPLIER & TRUCKING COMPANY: FAQs

What materials does Twisted Nail deliver?

We can supply any material sourced in the Texas Triangle, directly to your job site, including road base, limestone, granite, sand & gravel, river rock, fill & soil, and recycled materials. Whether your project calls for a small load or bulk aggregate delivered in volume, Twisted Nail sources the right material for the job.

What areas does Twisted Nail serve?

Twisted Nail is a Central Texas aggregate supplier and trucking company, serving Austin, Waco, San Antonio, Bryan, Killeen, Round Rock, Temple, and the surrounding region. Wherever your project sits in Central Texas, Twisted Nail can get material delivered to the site.

How does pricing work?

Twisted Nail uses simple pricing, with a single landed quote covering both material and delivery, so you’re not shopping for separate numbers from a supplier and a hauler. It’s a straightforward quote built around what your project actually needs.

Does Twisted Nail handle delivery, or just the material?

Both. Most quarries sell material but don’t offer delivery themselves, so Twisted Nail provides the full relationship, sourcing the material and handling the hauling in one call, rather than leaving you to coordinate two separate vendors.

What if I'm not sure my material will pass spec?

Twisted Nail manages the vendor vetting and testing process on your behalf. We coordinate sampling at the quarry, submit it to trusted testing facilities, and typically submit multiple samples at once to avoid delays. If a sample doesn’t pass, our supplier network lets us line up a fast replacement.

What kind of trucks does Twisted Nail run?

Twisted Nail’s fleet includes belly dumps, end dumps, steel round bottoms, tandems, and superdumps for aggregate hauling. For general freight, the fleet also runs flatbed and step deck trailers. Heavy and oversized loads are handled with stretch, lowboy, and RGN trailers.

Does Twisted Nail work with smaller or residential projects, or only large commercial jobs?

Twisted Nail supplies material for projects of every size, from single-building pads and parking lots to large-scale commercial work like data centers and solar and wind farms. Ranchers and residential property owners are also part of our customer base for smaller aggregate and fill orders.

Can Twisted Nail help with material haul-off or disposal?

Yes, Twisted Nail can source an approved disposal location as part of a full-service package, or handle the hauling alone if you already have a disposal site lined up. Either way, you get one point of contact for the job.

How do I get a quote from Twisted Nail?

Tell us what your plans call for, how much you need, and when. Twisted Nail sources the right material and coordinates delivery from there. Standard material quotes are typically returned within 1 business day, while commercial or specialty projects usually take 48–72 hours.

How do I become an owner-operator with Twisted Nail?

Twisted Nail works with owner-operators across our Central Texas network, offering competitive rates. If you’re interested in driving for Twisted Nail, visit our Drive For Us page.
